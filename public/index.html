How WordDuel Works

  • Modes: Play Solo to guess and solve at your own pace, challenge the Bot AI, or create an online room to duel a friend!
  • Same Word: In Duels, both players race to solve the exact same secret 5-letter word in real time.
  • Speed & Strategy: Guess the secret 5-letter word in 6 attempts. First to discover it wins the round!
  • Live Progress: In duels, your opponent's color pattern updates live on your screen as they make guesses (letters remain hidden).
  • Colors:
    Green: Right letter, right spot
    Yellow: Right letter, wrong spot
    Grey: Letter not in the word
  • Next Round / Next Word: Jump right into the next word and build your win streak!
